ScanWoke: Privacy Policy and Disclaimer

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This page explains how the ScanWoke Android app (“the app”) handles your information, and what the content in the app does and doesn’t represent.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, share or sell any personal or sensitive information. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not have permission to connect to the internet. All product information it shows is built into the app.

What you type into the search box and the barcodes you scan are used only on your device to find a matching entry. They are not saved or sent anywhere.

Barcode scanning

Scanning is provided by Google Play services, which opens its own camera screen. The app never receives camera images, only the number of the scanned barcode. Google Play services may collect limited diagnostic information about the scanner (such as device information, app information and error codes) to operate and improve the service. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

External links

Product pages link to articles on third-party websites. These open in your web browser. We don’t control those websites and aren’t responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Data retention and deletion

Because the app doesn’t collect personal information, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Uninstalling the app removes it from your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not affiliated with any brand

The app is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any company, brand or product it mentions. All product names, brand names and trademarks belong to their respective owners and are used only to identify the products discussed.

About the Woke score

The Woke score shown on some product pages is the app author’s opinion, based on the published articles listed under “References” on that page. It is not a statement of fact and says nothing about a product’s quality or safety.

Disclaimer

ScanWoke aggregates publicly available information, including published news articles and commentary, and presents it for general informational purposes only.

The inclusion of any product, brand or company in the app does not constitute a recommendation, endorsement or advice to purchase, or to refrain from purchasing, any product. Woke scores reflect the author’s opinion based on the referenced sources and should not be relied upon as a statement of fact.

Users are encouraged to review the original sources and to exercise their own independent judgement when making purchasing decisions.

Accuracy

Product names and barcodes come from public sources and may be incomplete or out of date. If you believe something in the app is inaccurate, please contact us and we will review it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
